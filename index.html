Questions

What if I don't know exactly what my leap is yet?

That's okay. Part of the work is getting clear. If something has been tugging at you, even if it's still blurry, that's enough to start.

How much time does it take?

The group classes are live on Wednesdays at 7 PM Perth time (7 AM New York, 12 PM London, 1 PM Madrid), across six weeks, plus two one-on-one sessions and the actions you take between them. It's designed to fit into a real life, not take it over.

What if I can't make all the live classes?

No problem. Every class is recorded, so you can watch back anytime and stay with the group at your own pace. And if anything needs explaining, or you get stuck between sessions, you can always message me. I'm here to support you throughout.

I've procrastinated on things like this before. Why would this be different?

Because you won't be doing it alone, and because we work on the fear underneath, not just the to-do list. Accountability and a group beside you do what willpower on its own can't.

Is it really just $225?

Yes. $225 per person for the full six weeks, with the four group classes and both private sessions included.

What do I actually walk away with?

Real change and real actions. A clear plan, momentum on the thing you've been avoiding, and the proof to yourself that you can move when it counts.
